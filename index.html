Built around two core uses

The site should help users follow both subjects and sources: what is being discussed, and who they have come to trust to interpret it.

1

Track expert analysis by topic

Each topic page should gather recent analysis from trusted voices on a single subject, whether that subject is the current war in Iran, a theological debate like Christian Zionism, or practical areas like fitness and nutrition.

2

Track the people behind the analysis

Each expert page should function like a living profile, showing that person’s latest appearances in media, new essays, interviews, podcast episodes, and connections to the topics where users most trust them.
